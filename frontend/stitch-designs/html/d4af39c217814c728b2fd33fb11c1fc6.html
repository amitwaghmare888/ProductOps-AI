Migrate core data-fetching from REST `api/v2/*` to GraphQL to solve over-fetching and latency issues identified in Q3 feedback.

## Scope
- `User`, `Organization`, and `PipelineRun` entities.
- Deprecation of legacy v1 endpoints.

## Out of Scope
- Mutations for settings configuration (retain REST for now).
